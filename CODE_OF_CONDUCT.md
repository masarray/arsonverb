# Code of conduct

ArSonVerb is a public free/open-source project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to collaborate professionally and respectfully.

- Discuss code, DSP, audio quality and test evidence constructively; criticism of decisions and reproducible bugs is welcome.
- No harassment, discriminatory attacks, intimidation, threats, doxxing or publication of another person's private information.
- Do not upload third-party copyrighted recordings, proprietary plugin code/presets, confidential logs or credentials without permission.
- Avoid spam, personal advertising and knowingly misleading claims of audio performance or certifications.

Maintainers may remove disruptive comments, close repetitive/off-topic reports or restrict participation when necessary, while keeping technical bug reports available for review.

To report conduct concerns privately, use GitHub's available private reporting facilities where appropriate. If none are available, request a private contact channel in a public issue **without describing sensitive personal details**. This document does not promise a 24-hour moderation or response SLA.
