**Algorithms:** Classic = former P3 signed Householder and bass contour. Plate = normalized 8x8 Hadamard butterfly; Chamber = four orthogonal cross-line Givens pairs; Hall = alternative Householder eigenvector. All share the original eight delay line buffers, diffusion, wet EQ, spatial control, presence ducking and stereo output. Their different mathematics yield measurably different tails, but not a claim that the Plate faithfully models a physical plate, or that Hall models a particular venue.

**JUCE state:** new four-choice algorithm parameter is appended after the preexisting 13 controls and bypass. First 13 numeric factory values, preset identifiers, C ABI and editor gold assets unchanged. New factory selections assign mode while older state files lacking it load Classic.

**Windows offscreen GUI:** 842x394 mean full paint 3.073ms / resize 7.154ms; 1180x545 mean 3.165ms / resize 9.678ms, 16 repetitions. Not an estimate of GPU/host frame rate.

**Field test:** use the unsigned Windows test package, compare Classic/Plate/Chamber/Hall at matched output level on vocal, snare, guitar and piano. Switch mode during tail, check for clicking/metallic ringing, sum to mono and compare stereo imaging. Record DAW and device/buffer settings for xrun and CPU. Visual approval at 100/125/150% scaling still open. For objective performance, repeat all 54 scenarios with explicit mode workload on the same physical machine; do not assume equal CPU cost across four matrices.
